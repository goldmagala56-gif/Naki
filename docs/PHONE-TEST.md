# Phone test round

Do this on the real phone, with headphones. Use a short MP4 clip (2 to 5 minutes) first.

## Setup
1. Open the Naki link in Chrome. Menu > Install app (or use the Install button on the home screen).
2. Open Naki from the home screen.
3. Tap **Check this device** and tap **Copy report**. Send me the text.

## Tests
| Test | What to do | What should happen |
|---|---|---|
| Record | New session, pick the clip, Record, talk over it for 2 minutes | Meter moves, voice records, movie lowers when you speak |
| Pause | While recording, pause the movie and keep talking for 20 seconds | Amber "Movie paused. Keep talking." shows, recording continues |
| Preview | Stop, then Play session | Picture, freezes and voice match what you did |
| Voice timing | If your voice is early or late, move the Voice timing slider | It lines up. Note the number |
| Offline | Turn on airplane mode, close Naki, open it, record 1 minute | Everything works |
| Interruptions | While recording, lock the screen for 10 seconds, then switch to another app and back | Naki tells you what happened. Note whether the recording kept going |
| Long session | Record 10 minutes | No lag, no big heat. Note the battery drop |
| File formats | Try one MP4 and one MKV if you have it | MP4 plays. Note what happens with MKV |

## Send me
- The Device check text.
- On the Preview screen, tap **Copy test report** after a finished session.
- Anything that felt awkward: buttons too small, confusing words, slow moments.
- If something broke: what you tapped, and what you saw.
